# what is networking ?
Networking is the process of connecting two or more computers or devices so that they can communicate and share data, resources, and information with each other.

[*] Networking ka matlab hai do ya do se zyada computers ya devices ko connect karna, taaki woh aapas mein data, information aur resources share kar saken.

# example-: 
Computer A → Switch → Router → Internet → Computer B
