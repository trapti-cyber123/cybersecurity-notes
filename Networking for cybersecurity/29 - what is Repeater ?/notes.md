# what is Repeater ?

  A Repeater is a network device that receives a weak signal, regenerates it, and retransmits it to extend the network distance or range.
  
  [*] Repeater ek network device hai jo weak signal ko receive karke regenerate/strong karta hai aur aage transmit karta hai. 
      Iska use network ki distance/range badhane ke liye hota hai.

  # Main Functions
  
  . Weak signal ko regenerate karta hai.
  
  . Network ki range/distance increase karta hai.
  
  . Signal ko further distance tak transmit karta hai.
  
  . Mostly Physical Layer (Layer 1) par work karta hai
